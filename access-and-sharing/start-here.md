# Access & Governance overview

Set up who can enter your app, what each person can do, and how you verify access before sharing it.

## Start with one complete access flow

1. Choose a [built-in role](teams-and-roles/built-in-roles.md) for an intended user.
2. [Invite that user by email](members-users-and-groups/invite-by-email.md) and confirm their team.
3. Configure [authentication](authentication-and-sso/) for the audience.
4. Review [page, action, and data permissions](app-and-data-permissions/).
5. [Test allowed and denied actions](overview/test-access.md) before publishing.
6. Use the [audit trail](audit-logs-privacy-and-security/review-the-audit-trail.md) and [system logs](audit-logs-privacy-and-security/investigate-system-logs.md) to investigate unexpected behavior.

## Four concepts to keep separate

**Authentication** establishes identity. **Membership and teams** associate people with the app. **Authorization** determines permitted actions and records. **Publishing** makes an app version available to its audience.

A successful sign-in does not prove that a user has the right permissions. A successful preview does not prove that a record-level restriction is enforced.
