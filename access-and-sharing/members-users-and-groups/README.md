# Users and invitations

Manage the people who use your app from **Users** in the builder navigation. The Users area includes **Users**, **Teams**, and **Authentication**.

## Choose a task

* [Review membership and user access](security-and-privacy.md) before adding or changing access.
* [Invite users by email](invite-by-email.md) when the invitation has a known recipient.
* [Invite users with a link](invite-with-a-link.md) when joining a selected team through a shared link is appropriate.
* [Import and export users](import-and-export-users.md) to work with user records in files.
* [Add user and team properties](user-and-team-properties.md) for attributes such as department or customer ID.
* [Set up teams and roles](../teams-and-roles/) to organize permissions.

## Before you invite anyone

Choose the team deliberately. The invitation dialog can show **Administrator** as its current selection; do not assume that this is the right role for app users.

Check the published app too. The invitation dialog warns that non-builder users see the published app, so an unpublished change may explain a difference between your builder and their experience.

Adding a user record, sending an invitation, and confirming that someone can sign in are separate checks. After onboarding, test with the intended account using [the access test checklist](../overview/test-access.md).
