# Members, users, and groups

Give each person the access needed for their job. A member who builds an app, a staff user who operates it, and a customer who sees only their own records may need different roles.

Start with [Members & Users](security-and-privacy.md), then use [User Groups](create-and-manage-a-team.md) when several people share the same permissions. Invite a test account in each role and confirm its visible pages, actions, and records. For detailed rules, continue with App and data permissions.
