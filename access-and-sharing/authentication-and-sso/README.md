# Authentication and SSO

Decide how users sign in before granting access to pages or data. [Authentication](authentication/) covers sign-in options; [App SSO](sign-in-sign-up/) covers single sign-on for an app.

Test sign-in with a user who should enter and one who should not. After authentication, check the person’s role and record permissions separately. For account and plan requirements, use [Subscription and Billing](../../account/subscription-and-billing.md) and the [current pricing page](https://www.jetadmin.io/pricing).
