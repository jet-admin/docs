# Explore core concepts

Start with an app task, then open the guide for the capability you need:

* [AI App Builder](https://app.gitbook.com/s/-LQ08RFAKZvFADEiXKFy/ai-app-builder): prompt, refine, test, and publish an app.
* [Data & Integrations](https://app.gitbook.com/s/-LQ08RFAKZvFADEiXKFy/user-guide): choose a source, connect it, and manage app data.
* [Workflows](https://app.gitbook.com/s/-LQ08RFAKZvFADEiXKFy/workflow): triggers, steps, schedules, approvals, and testing.
* [AI Agents](https://app.gitbook.com/s/-LQ08RFAKZvFADEiXKFy/ai-agents): instructions, tools, testing, and places agents can run.
* [Access & Governance](https://app.gitbook.com/s/-LQ08RFAKZvFADEiXKFy/access-and-sharing): sign-in, users, permissions, and audit logs.
* [Publish & Deploy](https://app.gitbook.com/s/-LQ08RFAKZvFADEiXKFy/jet-bridge-deployment): environments, sharing, and deployment.

[What can I build?](what-can-i-build.md) gives examples. [How credits work](../account/credits-and-rate-limits/how-credits-work.md) lives under Account.
