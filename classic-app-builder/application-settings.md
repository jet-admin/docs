# Application settings

Settings depend on the app and deployment you maintain. Confirm the app environment before changing a setting that affects users.

* [Account Settings](localization/account-settings.md) covers account-level options under Localization.
* [App settings](design-and-structure/core-concept/jet-ui/layouts-and-branding/) covers layouts and branding in a Classic app.
* [Environments](../jet-bridge-deployment/environments/) and [Version Control](../jet-bridge-deployment/environments-releases-and-version-control/version-control/) help control releases.
* [Custom domain](../jet-bridge-deployment/custom-domains/configuring-a-custom-domain.md) and [Authentication and SSO](../access-and-sharing/authentication-and-sso/) affect how users reach the app.

Preview as a representative user after changing appearance, sign-in, or a release setting. For programmatic configuration, use the [Jet Admin API](/broken/pages/-M2Ezm-MS-DTMS9hxOtE).
