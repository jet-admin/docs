8. Do not invent limits, supported file formats, pricing/plan eligibility, security guarantees, retention periods, recovery guarantees, or migration parity.
9. Documentation publication/readback validates content delivery, not successful product execution. Record what was inspected, what was tested, and what remains unverified.
