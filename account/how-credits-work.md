# How credits work

Jet Admin’s [current pricing page](https://www.jetadmin.io/pricing) describes AI credits as the allowance used when the AI Builder generates an app from a prompt or makes substantial edits. People using an app you have already built do not consume AI building credits simply by using it.

Plans differ in their monthly credit allowance. Free includes a limited allowance; Enterprise offers custom AI credits and lists rollover and on-demand top-ups. Check the pricing page and your account’s billing view for the allowance that applies to your plan.

## AI credit limits

A monthly credit allowance limits how much AI building work your plan includes. The pricing page does not state a fixed credit cost for each prompt. For a reliable current balance or cost, use the usage information shown in your Jet Admin account or ask support before a large build.

## Rate limits

A rate limit controls how quickly requests can be made; it is distinct from the monthly credit allowance. Jet Admin has not published a numerical AI Builder rate-limit schedule on its pricing page. If you are building an integration that depends on request throughput, ask Jet Admin support for the current limit for your plan and endpoint.
