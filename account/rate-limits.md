# Rate Limits

A rate limit controls the number of requests an endpoint accepts in a period of time. It is separate from your monthly AI credit allowance.

Jet Admin’s public pricing page does not specify numerical AI Builder or API rate limits. If your app or integration depends on sustained request volume, confirm the current limits for your plan and endpoint with Jet Admin support before deploying it. See [AI credit limits](ai-credit-limits.md) for monthly AI building usage.
