# Cancel Plan

To stop your Jet Admin plan from renewing, open **Billing & Plans** in your account and turn off automatic renewal. Jet Admin’s [current pricing FAQ](https://www.jetadmin.io/pricing) says you retain access through the end of the current billing period and there is no cancellation fee.

Review the renewal status in Billing & Plans after changing it. For plan changes or invoices, see [Subscription and Billing](subscription-and-billing.md).
