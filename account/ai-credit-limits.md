# AI credit limits

Your plan includes a monthly allowance for AI building. Jet Admin’s [current pricing page](https://www.jetadmin.io/pricing) says the Free plan has limited credits and Enterprise can have custom AI credits. The page does not list one fixed credit cost for every prompt.

AI credits are used when the AI Builder creates an app from a prompt or makes substantial edits. Simply using an already-built app does not spend these AI building credits. For a current allowance and balance, check your account’s billing and usage information.

See [How credits work](how-credits-work.md) for the usage model, and [Rate Limits](rate-limits.md) for request throughput.
