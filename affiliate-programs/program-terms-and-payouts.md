# Program terms and payouts

Jet Admin’s [Refer & Earn page](https://www.jetadmin.io/affiliate-refer-earn) currently advertises 30% of a referred customer’s subscription fees in their first year. The public page does not state a payout schedule, minimum payout, or all eligibility details.

Before promoting a rate or planning around a payment date, read the terms presented when you join and check your affiliate account for the current conditions. If you need a payout date, tax document, or an explanation of a referral’s status, contact the affiliate program team with your account details. For joining and tracking, see [Join and manage referrals](join-and-manage-referrals.md).
