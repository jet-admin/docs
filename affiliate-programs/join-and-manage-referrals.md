# Join and manage referrals

Open Jet Admin’s [Refer & Earn page](https://www.jetadmin.io/affiliate-refer-earn) and use its current application link. The page says affiliates sign up for a free account, receive a referral code, share it, and track earnings.

1. Apply through the official page and review the terms shown during signup.
2. Use the referral code provided to your affiliate account. Check that the link or code is correct before sharing it.
3. Track referred subscriptions and earnings in the affiliate system available to your account.
4. If a referral does not appear, keep the referral link, relevant dates, and account details so support can investigate.

For commission terms and payout details, see [Program terms and payouts](program-terms-and-payouts.md).
