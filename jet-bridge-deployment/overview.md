# Publish & Deploy overview

Take an app from a reviewed preview to a release your intended users can access.

## Release checklist

1. Confirm the app, environment, data connections, and audience.
2. Test pages, data actions, and permissions with representative accounts.
3. [Publish the reviewed app](publish-your-app/).
4. [Verify the published release](publish-your-app/verify-a-release.md) at its actual address.
5. [Share or embed the app](share-and-embed/) for the intended audience.
6. Record the released version and the [recovery approach](environments-releases-and-version-control/).

## Know which operation you need

**Preview** lets a builder inspect the app. **Publish** releases app changes for its audience. **Share** helps people reach or join the app. **Deployment** determines where its supporting services run.

The Jet Admin platform version, an app version in Version History, and an environment backup serve different purposes. Identify which one changed before troubleshooting or attempting recovery.
