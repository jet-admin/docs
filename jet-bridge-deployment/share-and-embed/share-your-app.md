# Share with the intended audience

Share the published app after deciding who should be able to use it and which team they should join.

## Invite a person

1. Select **Share** in the builder toolbar.
2. In **Invite with Email**, enter the intended recipient's email.
3. Select the appropriate team.
4. Review the recipient and team, then select **Send Invite**.
5. Confirm the resulting membership and test the recipient's published-app experience.

![Invite members dialog with email and team-based link invitation options](../../.gitbook/assets/02-invitations.png)

See [Invite users by email](../../access-and-sharing/members-users-and-groups/invite-by-email.md) and [Choose a built-in role](../../access-and-sharing/teams-and-roles/built-in-roles.md) for the detailed access workflow.

## Invite with a link

The same dialog contains **Invite with Link**, **Choose Team**, and **Add Invite Link**. Its notice states that anyone on the internet with the link can join the selected team.

Review the team's access before distributing such a link. Use [Invite users with a link](../../access-and-sharing/members-users-and-groups/invite-with-a-link.md) for the full procedure. Do not treat a team-joining invitation as proof that the app supports anonymous access.

## Verify what the recipient sees

The invitation dialog warns when there are unpublished changes: non-builder users see the published app. Check the actual address and signed-in account, then run [release verification](../publish-your-app/verify-a-release.md).

Use [social sharing settings](social-sharing-preview.md) only to change how the app address is presented on social platforms.
